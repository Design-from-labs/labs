# viscose-carousel: fidelity

- Upstream: [Yousuf-developer/Viscose-carousel `components/Carousel.jsx`](https://github.com/Yousuf-developer/Viscose-carousel/blob/f91a6de57019b7dcc58c28a2ea83f5a6dd9f3882/components/Carousel.jsx) with `components/ring/*.js` and `components/shaders/*.js`, at `f91a6de57019b7dcc58c28a2ea83f5a6dd9f3882` (MIT, copyright 2026 Yousuf Soomro; S-343), three.js 0.185.1 (S-275); its GSAP tweens are rebuilt on Motion (S-104), nothing from GSAP ships (S-345)
- Port: `ports/yousuf-viscose-carousel/viscose-carousel`, exported to `content/components/yousuf-viscose-carousel/viscose-carousel` (item `viscose-carousel`, primitive none, no icons)
- Renderer: Chromium (Playwright S-014) with SwiftShader software WebGL (`--use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader --disable-gpu-rasterization`, S-283), device scale 1, `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- Method: The upstream page runs as a production build (`next build && next start`) of a scratch copy whose eighteen projects are replaced by the port demo's own items (the same names, disciplines, years and generated SVG art, written to `public/` and `public/sm/` from the port page), and whose three faces (`Satoshi`, `Geist`, `PP Neue Montreal`) all point at the same Figtree variable file (`@fontsource-variable/figtree`, latin and latin-ext subsets) that the port harness uses as its only face. Both pages mount the carousel only once the face has loaded. The port renders the demo (`demo.tsx`, full-bleed at both viewports) in a Vite harness. Both pages run under Playwright's fake clock, paused while they load and then stepped in 16 ms frames (`clock.runFor(16)`), so every tween, the counter gate, the spin physics and the pointer springs advance identically and nothing waits on wall-clock time (P-060). A hook added after the name's lean in each page's loop records the ring's state every frame (`progress`, `launch`, `spread`, `spin`, `shift`, spin velocity, goo, wobble, counter, front card, hovered card); the traces are compared frame by frame before any pixel is. Screenshots are the whole viewport through the DevTools protocol (`Page.captureScreenshot`; Playwright's own screenshot waits for a frame the paused clock never gives), after 1.8 s of real time so the CSS transitions on both sides (the glow's colour, the fallback's fade) have finished. Diffs with pixelmatch (threshold 0.1, S-152). Noise floor: a second upstream load, diffed against the first.
- Scenario, per viewport: the intro at frames 50 (the seed's birth), 260 (the ring spreading, heading rising) and 420 (the stage move), the ring at rest after the entry and the name's melt, the pointer resting on the front card for 60 frames (the drop opens into the VIEW pill, the neighbours lean and dim), a scripted drag (8 pointer moves over 8 frames, released, 6 frames of momentum), then, five frames after it has snapped (while the name's melt still runs, so upstream's GSAP ticker is awake; see Deviations for a click after it has slept), the pointer over the side card above the front one for 40 frames and a click on it: the turn 16 and 32 frames in (the pick takes 35 frames; at its end upstream opens its project page, which the port hands to `onOpen`, so the comparison stops before it).
- Target: at most 0.1% differing pixels, or within the measured noise floor (owner brief, S-344).

Harness notes. The paused fake clock still advanced a millisecond or two while each page loaded, and each side starts its tween clock at a different moment of the load (upstream when GSAP's module loads, the port when its engine is built), so one run in three started the entry a frame apart. ## Deviations

Every change from upstream. The numbers above are measured on the shipped code, with these changes in place.

- **GSAP is rewritten with Motion.** Every tween is a Motion `animateValue` from 0 to 1 whose eased progress is written onto its targets (`viscose-carousel-motion.ts`); the eases are explicit functions equal to GSAP's named curves (power1 to power4, expo, circ, sine, back with its overshoot, none). The entry timeline keeps upstream's offsets, its pause at the seed (released by the counter through a delayed call), its staggered heading and its `onComplete`; `gsap.set`, `killTweensOf`, `delayedCall` and the pick and melt tweens map one to one. Values are rounded as GSAP rounds them (6 decimals for plain values, 4 for style values). Motion's driver is the carousel's own frame clock, ticked at the start of each drawn frame, as upstream's GSAP ticker ran before its three.js loop; a gap of more than 500 ms counts as 33 ms, as GSAP's lag smoothing does. The traces match upstream's frame for frame.
- **One upstream timing quirk is not reproduced.** When GSAP's ticker has gone to sleep (no tween for 120 frames) and a click wakes it, upstream's three.js loop runs before GSAP's ticker in every later frame, so its canvas draws each tween value one frame late (with GSAP's lag smoothing also shifting the start by about 2 ms). The port always applies tween values before it draws. Measured on a click after 120 idle frames at 1280x800: the turn differs by 5.424% 16 frames in and 0.064% 32 frames in (noise floor 0.000%; every earlier frame 0.000%); the table above clicks while upstream's ticker is awake, where the two match.
- **The project page is not ported; a card opens through `onOpen`.** Upstream's open swells the card into a hero and builds a whole page around it in the shader (its type rasterised to a sheet, its pictures on a second sheet, rules, a Close button, the next project's advance). That page is application content with its own data model (statement, insights, captioned media) and is not generic; the component calls `onOpen(item, index)` where upstream called `openProject`, after the same turn when a side card is clicked. The fragment shader is kept verbatim, its page uniforms bound and left at their "none" values, so the ring draws exactly as before.
- **No footage.** Upstream plays a card's own video loop once the ring rests on it; the port takes still images only (the clips were upstream assets and the shader's `uLive` stays bound to a blank texture).
- **No lil-gui panel.** The development panel is dropped; every one of upstream's tunables is a field of `params` (read when the ring is built), and `theme` picks the dark or light room.
- **Sized by its box, not the window.** The canvas fills the component and the render loop's ResizeObserver resizes it; everything upstream measured in `vw` or from `window.innerWidth` (the name's size, the index column, the glow's spread, the atlas cell width, the bands) is measured from the box's width in px. Elements upstream fixed to the viewport are absolute in the component, whose root carries the paper (`isolate`, so the name behind the ring still sits above it). The pointer's offset is measured with each pointer event, so the ring keeps working when the page scrolls.
- **Drawn through `useRenderLoop`.** three.js takes the loop's WebGL 2 context, created with the attributes three requests for upstream's `{ antialias: true, alpha: true }` (alpha, depth, no stencil, antialias, premultiplied alpha, no preserved buffer); the pixel ratio is capped at 2 (`maxDpr`); drawing stops off screen and in hidden tabs, and context loss is restored. Upstream's render-on-demand fingerprint is kept inside the loop's `render`. `forceContextLoss` is not called on dispose: the loop owns and releases the context.
- **Items instead of the project list.** `items` (`{ title, image, discipline?, year? }`) replace `PROJECTS` (`name`, `file`, `type`, `year`); up to 18 (the shader's thread budget, `MAX_LINKS`). Images are loaded with `crossOrigin = "anonymous"` (except data and blob URLs), since the sheet is uploaded to WebGL and read back for the glow; upstream's half-size `/sm/` copies are not used. The live region text drops a missing discipline or year.
- **Fonts.** Upstream named three files (Satoshi, Geist, PP Neue Montreal, the last commercial); none ships. `textFont`, `nameFont` and `idxFont` are CSS font-family lists that default to the component's own computed font, and the heading's fallback face param is gone. Before building the canvas heading and the drop's word the port also waits for those faces to load (`document.fonts.load`), where upstream waited for `document.fonts.ready` only.
- **Static fallback.** Before the first frame, without WebGL 2 and under reduced motion (upstream had no reduced-motion path) a still shows instead: the resting arc of cards in inline SVG with the front project's name, in its own layer over the canvas, faded out once the loop reports `ready`. `fallback` replaces it. Under reduced motion the arrow keys and Enter still work on the still.
- **Keyboard and screen readers.** The ring is a focusable `listbox` whose options name each project (title, discipline, year) with `aria-activedescendant` on the front one: arrow keys turn the ring a card at a time (a pick that does not open), Home and End go to the ends, Enter and Space open the front card. Upstream's index column (the `ul` it exposed as "Projects") and the melting lockups are `aria-hidden`; the polite live region is kept. A focus ring shows on keyboard focus only.
- **CSS moved inline, names prefixed.** Upstream's `globals.css` rules for the room (vignette, glow, dither) and the grain are inline styles; `--paper`, `--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--glow*`, `--vig` and `--dither` are `--viscose-carousel-*`; the glow colour is registered with `CSS.registerProperty` (upstream `@property`); the grain's blend mode is set directly instead of through `data-blend`; the SVG filter id is per instance.
- **Renames.** `Carousel` is `ViscoseCarousel`, `defaultParams` is `viscoseCarouselDefaults`, `THEMES` is `VISCOSE_THEMES`; the effect body is `createViscoseCarouselEngine`. Merged modules keep their names inside `viscose-carousel-engine.ts` behind `// ---- upstream: <path> ----` markers; the shaders are verbatim in `viscose-carousel-shaders.ts`.
- **Removed with the cut features:** the dev panel's readouts (`info`), the open, close, advance, hero and media state, the footage state, the Escape and keyboard-focus (`data-keys`) handlers that served the page, and the open's terms in the layout (`openS`, the flee, the pulse, the open's melt and wobble), each of which is zero or one without an open page, so the arithmetic of the ring is unchanged.
