## Deviations from upstream (the default look is unchanged)

- The pen's `.elevation-N` and `.dm-elevation-N` classes are `[data-slot="elevation"][data-level="N"]` (same specificity); each level holds the pen's light list and dark list as two internal variables, and a short rule picks one (`mode`). The CSS ships in a hoisted `<style href="designfrom-elevation">` (React 19).
- The pen's tokens are `--elevation-*` variables with the pen's values as fallbacks and in `cssVars`: `--shadow-color` is `--elevation-shadow-color`; `--dm-hi-base|mid|high|peak` are `--elevation-dark-hi-*`; `--dm-ring-base|mid|high` are `--elevation-dark-ring-*`; `--dm-drop` is `--elevation-dark-drop`. The literal rings on the dark scale (black at 12% to 20%) stay literal, as upstream.
- The component sets only the shadow. The pen's `.dm-card` and `.lm-card` (10rem square, 1.5rem radius, `#262626` or white) are class names on the demo and the harness, not the component.
- Level 7 exists only on the pen's dark scale. On the light scale the component repeats level 6 rather than invent a layer.
- The source's attribution notice is `Copyright (c) 2026 Josh Miller, Shadow Stacking I (a fork of Shadow Stacking II). MIT License.`, without the pen URLs, because a long URL in the notice widened the component page at 320px (P-WEB1-A2-5); both URLs are in `ports/jshmllr-shadow-stacking/LICENSE` (appended to every exported file) and in the credits (S-461, S-462).
- The gallery demo is the pen's two scales, each card the pen's square at a smaller size (4rem) and labelled with its level, in two sections (the pen's dark section, then its light one) sized for the wall tile and the 1200x750 still. It leaves out the pen's per-card layer lists and role names (they are in the props and the pen's source) and uses the repo's mono font. The fidelity comparison above uses the pen's own page, labels and layout included.

## Enhancements (after fidelity)

- **Mode**: `mode="light"` or `"dark"` picks a scale outright; the default, `"auto"`, follows the theme (`.dark`, `[data-theme="dark"]`, or the system when no theme is set).
- **Motion**: the shadow eases between levels over 200 ms, only when reduced motion is not requested.
- **Right to left**: nothing to mirror: the shadows have no horizontal offset.
- **Contrast on request** (fidelity default, contrast on request; `ports/README.md`): the pen's rings are 2% to 6% white on dark and 6% black on light, far from the 3:1 a visible edge needs. Under `prefers-contrast: more` every surface gets a 1px edge at 60% black (5.74:1 on white) or 60% white; under `forced-colors: active` a 1px `CanvasText` outline replaces the removed shadows (not at level 0). The colors are `--elevation-edge-light` and `--elevation-edge-dark`.
- **Screen reader**: the root is a plain `div` with no role; elevation is decoration. axe (WCAG 2.2 AA and best practices) on `/ui/elevation`, light and dark, with more contrast requested, and after switching to right to left: no violations (`e2e/shadow-ports.spec.ts`).
- **Phones**: the demo's cards shrink to fit (`max-w-full`); checked at 320 and 375px, no horizontal page scroll.
